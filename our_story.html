Our Story

Founder and CEO: Aja Murray

A Town Taps is inspired by Atlanta culture and reflective of the way this city brings people together. Culture on Tap celebrates the beauty, people, history, and stories that connect Atlanta through locally crafted drinks in a city that influences culture globally and sets the blueprint.


At the heart of A Town Taps is the flagship mobile beverage bike Red, named after founder Aja Murray’s late grandmother Gwen Moye, whose nickname was Red. She was a small town dirt road homegrown woman, a protector, a mother, and someone who carried joy wherever she went. She believed in standing up for what was right and lived by the idea that “y’all means all.” If there was a celebration, you could count on her to throw a damn good party. She was the life of every gathering and the matriarch at the heart of her family.


That same spirit lives on in Red, Atlanta’s first mobile beverage bike. Bold, rooted, resilient, welcoming, and built to bring people together.


Her spirit continues through A Town Taps and guides its commitment to community and the spirit of Good Trouble, creating spaces where connection and celebration can happen.
