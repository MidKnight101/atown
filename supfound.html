Supporting Founders

A-Town Taps was brought to life by a community that believed in the vision of bringing Culture on Tap to Atlanta. Supporting Founders are the early champions who helped make the launch of Red, Atlanta's first mobile beverage bike, possible. We are grateful for the people who believed in A-Town Taps from the very beginning.
